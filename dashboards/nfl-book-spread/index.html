Book Spread

Where the sportsbooks disagree most on a player's fantasy projection, for this week.

Where the books disagree. Each dot is one sportsbook's projection for the player, scored half-PPR from that book's own lines. Players are sorted by the gap between the lowest and the highest book, so the top of the list is where shopping for a number matters most.
How the dots are worked out

A book's dot uses every line that book posted for the player: yardage, receptions, passing touchdowns and its anytime-touchdown price, turned into expected touchdowns. Where a book posted no line for a stat, the consensus (the median across books) stands in, so a dot moves only on what that book actually priced.

The hollow dot is Kalshi, a prediction market rather than a sportsbook. The thin mark is the consensus, and every row uses the same scale, so a wide row really is a wider disagreement. Only players priced by at least three sources, in games that have not kicked off, are listed.
