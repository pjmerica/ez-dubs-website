Vegas Projections

Every player with a betting line for this week, and the fantasy points those lines imply.

Every player the betting markets have priced, scored from those lines. Each stat is the sportsbooks' consensus line, with Kalshi filling any stat no book posted, and the points are those lines scored in the format you pick. No season projection is mixed in: what you see is what the markets imply.
How the projections are worked out

Every number is a betting line. Yardage is the median of the books' lines for that stat. Receptions and passing touchdowns are the expected count the books' line and price imply, since books post different half-point lines at very different prices. TDs is expected rushing plus receiving touchdowns, from the anytime-touchdown price. Where no book posted a stat, Kalshi's line stands in. Hover a number for how many books priced it and their range.

The points are the standard scoring of those lines: a point per 10 rushing or receiving yards, per 25 passing yards, 6 per rushing or receiving touchdown and 4 per passing touchdown, plus a point per catch in PPR or half a point in half PPR. Interceptions and fumbles are not counted, because no market prices them.

A stat no market priced is a dash, and counts as zero. Most players have only a touchdown line, which on its own is a few points, so they are hidden until you tick the box above; Start/Sit fills their yardage from a season projection instead. A game that has kicked off shows its last lines before kickoff.
